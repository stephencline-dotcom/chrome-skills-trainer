Practice Window Controls! Practice Preview
  1. 1 Click Minimize (➖) to hide Chrome into the taskbar.
  2. 2 Click the Chrome taskbar button to bring it back.
  3. 3 Click Maximize (🔲) or double-click title bar to expand.
  4. 4 Click Restore Down (❐) to shrink it back.
  5. 5 Click and drag the title bar to move the window.
  6. 6 Click Close (❌), then click the taskbar button to open a new window!
